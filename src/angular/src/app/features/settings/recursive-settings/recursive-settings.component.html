Names at or below one of these domains are not validated with DNSSEC: the answer is served as insecure, never with the AD bit, and never fails for DNSSEC reasons. Use it for private zones that the public DNSSEC chain says do not exist (for example corp.example). A conditional forwarder zone has its own switch. One domain per line. The built-in list holds home.arpa (RFC 8375), internal and the locally-served reverse zones of RFC 6303 and RFC 7793; edit it freely, and use "Restore defaults" to go back. Takes effect at once.
